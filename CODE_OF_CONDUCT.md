# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community. Good participation includes:

- assuming good intent while addressing evidence and impact;
- giving specific, actionable technical feedback;
- welcoming questions and different levels of experience;
- respecting privacy, boundaries, and attribution;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and repairing harm.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, releases, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile or an appropriate private repository channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban participants when needed to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is not acceptable.
