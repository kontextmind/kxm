## Risks and guards

- Billing Google via OpenRouter: guard is vendor/harness pairing lint plus the roster test. Refuse any `openrouter/google/...` writer entry.
- Silent fallthrough: `agy -p` exits on unavailable model; the runner must treat exit as route failure and move to the next eligible route, not retry via Pi. Refuse adding a Pi `google` provider route.
- Plan gating: Argon needs a paid plan; logged-in agy on a free plan still fails. Guard: `kxm harness list` auth check remains fail-closed; record the failure as cost-unknown attempt.
- Price staleness: dated tiers avoid underquoting after 2026-11-30; hash pin must be regenerated by script, never hand-edited.
- Inventory refresh dropping CLI rows: covered by the preservation test.
- Refuse: harness preferences overlay, long-lived agy worker, putting Argon on planner/review rosters, changing `kxm init` defaults (grok-4.6/fable), dual names or aliases.
