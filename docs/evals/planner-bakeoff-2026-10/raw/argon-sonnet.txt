## Risks and guards
- Argon needs a paid plan. If the account lacks it, `agy -p` exits and does not fall through. Treat that exit as a route failure and hand over to the next eligible authenticated model. Do not retry silently on a different vendor.
- The price step on 2026-11-30 makes the dated pin stale. Record the effective-until date in the row or in the docs. A stale catalog must not silently underquote.
- Do not pin a base `gemini-4-argon` plus an effort flag. The packet only supports the three pinned names, so use the pinned name.
- If the refresh is a naive overwrite, it drops the Argon row on this machine.
- Do not edit the hash by hand. Regenerate it.
- Refuse these: a Google route through OpenRouter or Pi, a harness preferences overlay, declaring agy a long-lived worker, dual or alias names, and Argon on the planner or review rosters.
