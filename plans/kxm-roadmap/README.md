# Roadmap

Edit `state.json` only. `dashboard.md`, `MASTER.md`, and `phases/` are generated by `update-dashboard.mjs`.

Schema: [`schemas/roadmap-state.schema.json`](../../schemas/roadmap-state.schema.json).

1. Remove a fact that no longer holds. A contradiction is worse than a gap.
2. History is one short line per material change, at most 12. The generator drops the oldest.
3. A contact has an owner role and a confirmed date. Drop it after 90 days. Empty is fine.
4. Keep present state and the next phases. Delete a superseded phase. Do not archive it here.
5. A far phase stays brief and complete. Near work gains what a writer needs, then a brief.
6. A task names the artifact template it follows: `feature`, `research`, `bug-fix`, `architecture`, `adr`, `test-plan`, `test-report`, `review`, `handoff`, `runbook`, `postmortem`, or `none`.
